JUDGE DEFENSE PANEL & REGULATORY CONTEXT

Direct technical answers to key hackathon questions and MARPOL Annex I compliance alignment.

INTERNATIONAL MARITIME ORGANIZATION (IMO) — MARPOL ANNEX I ALIGNMENT

"Ships have strict reporting and pollution-prevention obligations under MARPOL Annex I. OilGuard AI does not replace that process. Our system focuses on the authority-side intelligence gap by combining available vessel, imagery, satellite and environmental evidence to help verify possible incidents, assess potential spread, and identify potentially affected coastal areas faster."

Q1: How do you get data from a foreign ship?
"We do not access a foreign vessel's private systems. The prototype uses simulated AIS/VTS data. In real deployment, authorities would use authorized maritime surveillance feeds. Private engine telemetry would only be available through an authorized data-sharing agreement or port state control protocol."
Q2: Can AIS alone detect an oil spill?
"No. AIS provides vessel identity, speed, course, and movement information. OilGuard uses vessel behavior (such as unexpected deceleration or abnormal maneuvering) purely as an investigation trigger, which it then correlates with other independent evidence such as optical imagery, satellite SAR observations, and incident reports."
Q3: What if the ship doesn't report the spill?
"OilGuard can still support investigations using independently available maritime surveillance, drone/patrol imagery, and satellite observations. The platform supports both report-first and independent-observation workflows, without claiming to detect every spill automatically."
Q4: Is the satellite feed continuous live video?
"No. Sentinel-1 SAR is a Synthetic Aperture Radar sensor, not live continuous video. OilGuard uses the latest available observation pass as corroborating evidence when an observation is available."
Q5: Why use an AI Fusion Engine?
"Marine authorities are overwhelmed by fragmented, multi-modal data streams. The AI Fusion Engine correlates heterogeneous evidence (AIS vectors, telemetry anomalies, optical textures, SAR radar backscatter, and environmental sensitivity) to calculate an explainable priority score for human inspectors."
Q6: Can this platform replace human maritime authorities?
"No. OilGuard is purely a decision-support platform. Human and authority verification is a mandatory, uncompromising step in the workflow before any on-scene containment or legal enforcement action is initiated."
Q7: Can the spill prediction model be trusted operationally?
"The hackathon model is a simplified prototype trajectory simulation based on empirical windage and surface current vectors. Production deployment would require coupling with validated operational oceanographic hydrodynamic models (e.g., GNOME, OpenDrift) and authoritative metocean forecasts."
